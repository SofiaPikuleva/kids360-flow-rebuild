What this is

We went through Kids360’s first run the way a new parent does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Kids360: went through all eighteen screens before the price — the goal picker, the five yes/no questions, the feature slides, the reviews, the loader — then the paywall and the “Don’t leave yet” sheet, and read your App Store listing.

What follows is five ideas — not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours — the purple gradient welcome, the lavender canvas and white rounded cards, the goal tiles with your illustrations, the progress bar with Skip, the ring loader, the plan cards with the “Save 50%” tab and the crocodile sheet. Only the order and a few lines changed.

Everything is already yours

The goals, the feature slides, the review, the 2.5x survey line, the prices and the 3-day trial all come from your own screens. The rating comes from your App Store page. We added no new claims.
